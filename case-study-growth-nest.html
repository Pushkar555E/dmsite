Challenge

A clearer online presence for a digital marketing agency.

Growth Nest needed a clear online presence that could explain its services, present a professional digital identity and make it easy for potential clients to get in touch.

Objectives

  • Present digital marketing services clearly
  • Create a professional visual identity
  • Build a responsive experience
  • Create visible contact actions
  • Organize content for easy scanning
  • Prepare the site for online deployment
Work completed

Website structure, presentation and contact flow.

Website structure

Navigation and page sections organize services, industries, pricing, blog, about and FAQ content.

Hero and service sections

The homepage leads with a clear growth-focused headline and service-oriented positioning.

Calls to action

The site uses a visible free-audit action and enquiry-focused pathways.

Brand presentation

Logo, typography, soft grid background and cyan-blue accents create a consistent agency identity.

Mobile layout

A mobile-size version is included to show how the project appears on a narrow screen.

Deployment

The project is live on Vercel at growth-fawn-two.vercel.app.

Design decisions

Simple hierarchy and direct actions.

The design uses a spacious hero, service-led navigation, a strong free-audit CTA, and clean typography to make the agency offer easy to scan. The browser-style previews below show real captures from the live website.

Outcome

  • Live website successfully deployed
  • Responsive presentation created
  • Services organized clearly
  • Contact routes made visible
  • Brand given a consistent online presentation
