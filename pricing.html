Pricing FAQ

Questions before requesting a quote.

Pricing and payment

Are these final prices?

No. They are starting prices. Final quotations depend on project scope, number of pages, content, integrations, delivery timeline and support requirements.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain, hosting, premium plugins, paid tools and advertising budgets are separate unless stated otherwise.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. A written project scope and final quotation will be shared before work begins.

How much advance payment is required?

Projects below ₹5,000 require 60% advance. Projects of ₹5,000 and above require 50% advance.

Websites and delivery

How long does a website take?

Basic landing pages may take 4-6 working days. Business websites usually take 8-14 working days after content is ready.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds depend on the package. Additional revision rounds can be purchased as an add-on.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. Additional website pages start at ₹699 per page.

Marketing expectations

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. Search rankings cannot be guaranteed because they depend on competition, search engines and ongoing work.

Is advertising spend included?

No. Advertising budget is paid separately by the client directly to Meta, Google or the relevant platform.

Can I choose only one service?

Yes. You can request one service such as a landing page, SEO audit, logo design or maintenance.

Support and maintenance

Do you work with businesses outside West Bengal?

Yes. Nexora Digital serves local clients around Ichhapur, Barrackpore, Kolkata and remote clients across India.

What happens after the support period?

You can request maintenance, small updates, or a new support package after the included support period.

Is GST included?

Any applicable taxes will be clearly stated in the final quotation.

Can I pay in instalments?

Payment schedules can be discussed for larger scopes, but work begins after the agreed advance is received.

What content must I provide?

You should provide business details, images, service information, legal content, brand assets, product details and timely approvals.
