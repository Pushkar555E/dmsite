Growth Nest

Overview: Growth Nest was a completed client website/brand presentation project. The original live deployment is no longer publicly available, so the uploaded proof images are shown as an archive.

Challenge: Present the work clearly and guide visitors toward the right next action.

Objectives: Clear service positioning, Logo-based brand presentation, Mobile-friendly layout, Contact-oriented CTA flow.

My role: Website builder and brand presentation support.

Process: Review, structure, design, build, test, and prepare launch/archive proof.

Final outcome: Completed archived project proof added, Client work clearly separated from internal projects.
