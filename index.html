FAQ

Common questions before starting.

How much does a website cost?

Starter landing pages begin from ₹4,999 and small business websites begin from ₹9,999. Final pricing depends on scope, content, integrations, timeline, and support requirements.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 5-7 days. A small business website usually takes 10-14 days after content and requirements are clear.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting are usually billed separately. Nexora Digital can guide setup or include support in a custom scope.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Redesign work can improve layout, mobile responsiveness, calls to action, content structure, and SEO basics.

Do you work with local businesses?

Yes. Nexora Digital works with small businesses, startups, local service providers, and growing brands in West Bengal and remotely across India.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes. Nexora Digital provides SEO foundations such as metadata, page structure, local SEO setup, Google Business Profile guidance, and Search Console setup.

Can you manage social media?

Basic social media planning and starter support are available. Larger content production can be scoped separately.

How many revisions are included?

Most website packages include two revision rounds. Small monthly support plans include agreed minor updates.

What information is needed to begin?

Your business details, goals, services, contact information, brand assets, existing website if any, and preferred timeline are helpful.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Support is included for the period listed in each package, and monthly maintenance is available.

Can I request only one service?

Yes. You can request one service such as a landing page, SEO setup, audit, or maintenance.

What happens if my previous website is unavailable?

Archived screenshots or proof images can be used to document completed work without adding broken live demo links.
